# WorkingThroughClockwork

A Victorian-inspired retro platformer built in python.

## Overview

Explore abandoned ruins filled with forgotten machinery, dangerous platforms, and lost artifacts. 

### To Win:
Find 3 objects left behind by an ancient civilization: a pocketwatch, a hat, and a golden cog.

**Theme:** Antique — Victorian machinery, brass mechanisms, clockwork, old factories, and ruins. Lots & Lots of gears

## Game:
- Plays like a simple platformer
- Collect the objects.
- Avoid hazards and learn new mechanics.
- Reach the end of each level to progress.

## Mechanics:
- Regular platformer
- Second level is more timing based, after solving a puzzle(clicking on a gear until it moves into the right place kind of like a captcha), you only have 30 seconds to clear the next room? 
- Respawn points
- Simple pve where wild mechanical rats or something chase you in certain rooms and you have to get to the middle of the room and stand in a safe zone.
- Ceiling crumbles in certain rooms so dodging

## Levels:
### Level 1 — Abandoned Workshop
Regular platformer, you get the golden cog at the end. A workshop filled with old machinery. 1 gear clicking puzzle to open up the middle of the room to get the cog.

### Level 2 — Overgrowth
More environmental obstacles. The mechanical rats go here. Harder level in general. Collapsing ceiling and mini maze puzzle? - a 4x4 grid maze with 1 very easy path, but you have to solve in less than 30 seconds to get out before the map breaks. random orientation(up, down, left, right, because it'll still have one start and end and be solvable). You get the black hat here.

### Level 3 — The Clocktower
The hardest level. Just more complicated. You get the pocketwatch. Puzzle where you have to stop moving at certain parts as the screen shakes and the clock ticks forward or backwards(if you move a dinosaur eats you). Moving platforms in this one for sure.

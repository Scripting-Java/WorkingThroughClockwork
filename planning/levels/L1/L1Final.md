# Level 1 

## 1. Overview
*Abandoned Workshop*  
Theme: An abandoned Victorian-era workshop/factory with broken machinery, steampunk mechanisms,  offices, and a failing boiler system-> Main plot element w/ floor failing and turning it off.

**How to clear:** Move through the workshop, shut down the boiler, and collect the black hat from the office on Floor 2.
